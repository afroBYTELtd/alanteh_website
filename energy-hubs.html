Energy Hubs

Energy hubs that keep the service moving.

Charging, parking and service locations that support cleaner daily transport.

Hub role

A clean ride service needs a reliable home base.

Energy hubs keep vehicles close to the people and places they serve. Each hub supports charging access, parking, basic checks and day to day service readiness.

For riders, this means cleaner journeys. For partners, it means a more organized mobility service.

Location
