Energy

Solar power for electric fleets.

ALANTEH operates solar powered and grid supported charging and battery exchange infrastructure designed for African grid conditions and fleet operations. Vehicle solar roofs support onboard systems and parked battery top up, while the main battery delivers the core drive power.

Hub role

A clean mobility service needs a reliable energy base.

Energy hubs keep vehicles close to the people and places they serve. Each hub combines solar power, electric grid support, charging access, battery exchange, parking, basic checks and day to day service readiness.

For passengers, operators, and partners, this means cleaner journeys and a more organized mobility service powered by a practical solar assisted charging and battery exchange model. Solar is a major support layer across vehicle roofs, parked top ups, and hub energy planning, while batteries provide dependable daily operation.
