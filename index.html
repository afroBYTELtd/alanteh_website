How it works

One integrated system.

01Book

Passengers book a ride through the ALANTEH platform. Pickup and destination confirmed.

02Dispatch

Our operations team assigns a driver. The trip is GPS tracked from pickup to drop off.

03Pay

Payment via mobile money. No cash or card machine required.

04Charge

Vehicles return to solar assisted hubs. Solar roofs support onboard systems and parked battery top up while hub power prepares the next shift.

05Scale

The same system rolls out city by city as ALANTEH expands across Africa.
