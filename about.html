About

Built for Africa. Starting in Ghana.

ALANTEH is a solar supported electric mobility company. We develop and operate the technology, vehicles, and energy infrastructure that make clean transport work in African cities.

Company

We own and operate the full system.

Most transport businesses outsource the hard parts. ALANTEH does not.

We own the vehicles, build the technology platform, manage the drivers, and operate the solar supported energy infrastructure. Every part of the service is under our direct control because that is the only way to guarantee reliability in African markets.
